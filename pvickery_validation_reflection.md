# Validation Reflection — pvickery

Across the ten projects validat from cboddick, we had 9 disagreements out of 50 fields.
HasRecovered,CurrentStatus, and RecentThemes had the least disagreements, with each having
one disagreement. WhyRecovered had 2 disagreements, and ActivityPattern had the most disagreements
with 4 total. These disagreements were due to differing use of the irregular and declining label,
but we were able to come to an agreement with each of these discrepancies.

One trend I noticed was that long gaps when renewed tended to be due to maintenance or documentation updates,
rather than significant feature development. Developers would often return after a long gap to do bug fixes, update
dependencies, and improve documentation.

The most difficult part during validation was the less objective fields such as ActivityPatterns and WhyRecovered.
It is easier to agree on more objective fields such as HasRecovered and CurrentStatus, as those have set definitions.
ActivityPatterns was the most difficult as we sometimes had different interpretations of the labels used. However, after
discussion about each of these discrepancies, we were able to come to a more clear understanding of each of the labels. This allowed
us to have a much more clear understanding of the activity patterns.
