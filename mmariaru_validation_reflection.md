# Validation Reflection

After looking at these ten projects, I noticed that most of the gaps happened in projects where activity was already declining. DAPPER was more irregular. Mcx had a U-shaped pattern. Kedro-Viz was different because it did not have a gap of at least three months, and its activity was rising.

The projects usually returned because there was a reason to work on them again. Sambamba, Toppra, Probe, and MCX needed compatibility or dependency changes. N2P2 came back for prediction and LAMMPS features. Other projects returned for documentation bug fixes or smaller maintenance work. A long gap did not always mean a project was dead. Probe returned after twenty-three months, and N2P2 returned after about fifteen months.

Siddh and I agreed on everything before our discussion. Our only disagreement was the themes for BISWEB. The original value included bot contributions. The latest default-branch commits showed code and several fixes, so we agreed on Feature development and Bug fixes.

This showed me that recent themes should be based on the latest commits, not only the general history of the repository. Clear rules also helped with the fields. We used the three-month gap rule and the April 1, 2025 cutoff in the way. After discussing the BISWEB evidence, we reached agreement on all fifty rows.
